- Closest framework: Sattelberger-van der Veer, "Maximum likelihood estimation from a tropical and a Bernstein-Sato perspective", arXiv:2101.03570 (IMRN, rnac016). Prop. 2.10: along a curve of critical points of prod f_i^{alpha_i} tending to boundary divisors E_j of an SNC compactification, alpha lies in each H_{E_j} = {sum_i ord_{E_j}(f_i) alpha_i = 0}; Cor. 3.3 and the remark before it give ord_t(gamma^* f_i) = sum_j ord_{E_j}(f_i) for the tropical (schon) setting.
