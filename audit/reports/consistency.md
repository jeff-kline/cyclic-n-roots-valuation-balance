## Plain-language suggestions for the README (newcomer, max 5)

1. Lines 13-17 (intro): "disjoint supports in time and frequency" and "such pairs" use "support" and "time/frequency" before defining them. Define once: the support of a vector is the set of its nonzero coordinates; "frequency" means the coordinates of its Fourier transform.
2. Lines 19-22: "Along any Laurent-series solution ... the orders of vanishing ... agree as multisets; ... a residue principle for critical points of master functions". The intro never writes the Fourier equations, and "master function" is never defined (a product of powers of linear forms). Add the equation or a one-line description of the Fourier form first, then drop or gloss the residue-principle clause.
3. Lines 25-29: "a boundary point", "first two Taylor conditions" and "endpoints" are undefined. Say what is being tested: whether a solution branch (a curve of solutions) can leave the point; kappa=0 is the first necessary condition for a branch to exist.
4. Lines 47-53 (first bullet): one sentence carries the equation, the cut, four support conditions and a historical remark. Split into the hypothesis (a solution with all coordinates nonzero), the cut (a real number c that is not one of these orders), and the conclusion (I, J, u, v), and define F as the unnormalized Fourier matrix.
5. Lines 55-59 (second bullet): "a nondegenerate critical point of the associated product of linear forms" and "in the given directions" refer to objects the README never introduces. Replace with a description of the setup (a boundary point u with a one-dimensional kernel, and a first correction direction w) or point to the paper section.
