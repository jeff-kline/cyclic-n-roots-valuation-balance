# Bounded source comparison — 2026-10-26

Status: PARTIAL. Absence from this small comparison is not a novelty result.

Primary sources opened and compared:

1. U. Haagerup, Cyclic p-roots of prime length p and related complex Hadamard
   matrices, arXiv:0803.2629:
   https://arxiv.org/html/0803.2629
   Supplies the Fourier formulation, limiting-support framework, and
   prime-order finiteness. Those are credited, not claimed as new.
2. W. Zhou, On number of cyclic n-roots and disjointness of Fourier supports,
   arXiv:2608.06335v2:
   https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06335v2
   Theorem 1 gives disjoint-support pairs for every composite order.
   It does not state this draft's valuation multiset equality or the
   simultaneous every-gap exact-complement conclusion.
3. D. Cohen, G. Denham, M. Falk, A. Varchenko, Critical points and resonance
   of hyperplane arrangements, Canadian Journal of Mathematics 63 (2011),
   1038–1057:
   https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.0896
   https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-2011-028-8
   Abstract and bibliographic record checked for master-function context;
   this was not a full theorem-by-theorem reading of that paper.

The derivative identity, every-gap consequence, explicit scalar formulas,
and order-26 cancellation are the proposed contribution. The general
implicit-function construction and Fourier affine symmetries are standard.

Still needed before asserting novelty: equivalent formulations in tropical
critical loci, reciprocal linear spaces, and logarithmic/master-function
geometry; closest prior local boundary analyses of cyclic roots; original
source-level attribution of the affine symmetry group. The user reported a
separate D3 bounded scan, but its packet was not inspected in this quarantine.
